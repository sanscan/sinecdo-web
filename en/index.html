03 / Fit

🧩Sinecdo works best when there is already something valuable to bring into alignment.

We are not trying to fill a calendar. We look for conversations where we can contribute sound judgment.

A good fit

  • +A company, studio, consultancy or specialist professional with an active offer.
  • +There is traction, a client base, experience or real project work.
  • +There are current materials to review.
  • +There is a problem with clarity, perception, positioning or sales.
  • +A decision-maker is involved.
  • +There is a willingness to invest when a concrete opportunity emerges.

Not a priority

  • ×Early-stage ideas without a validated offer.
  • ×Requests limited to a standalone logo, flyer or social post.
  • ×Wanting to “do marketing” without reviewing the offer or message.
  • ×Comparisons based solely on price.
  • ×No materials or context to support a diagnosis.
  • ×Expecting a complete audit for free.
