Privacy notice

Sinecdo uses the information you choose to share to record your case, respond to requests and manage a potential commercial relationship. You do not need to register to see your quiz result.

Information we may receive

Name, email, company or brand, role, sector, website, WhatsApp and the answers you submit through the quiz or a form. We may also retain attribution data such as campaign, medium and entry page. We do not use IP addresses as marketing identifiers.

Why we use it

  • to record and understand the case you submit;
  • to respond when you ask to be contacted or request a session;
  • to review applications before sharing a booking link;
  • to maintain operational traceability and prevent duplicate submissions.

Sharing a result does not subscribe you to newsletters or campaigns. Any future marketing subscription will require a separate choice.

Providers and storage

Our operation may use Google services for internal records and Hostinger for website and email infrastructure. Those providers may process information according to their own terms and Sinecdo's configuration. In this version we do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers or free-text answers to general analytics tools.

Retention and security

We retain information while it is necessary for the stated purpose, to manage an active relationship or to meet applicable obligations. We periodically review records that no longer have an active purpose. We apply reasonable access and data-minimisation controls, although no Internet-connected system can guarantee zero risk.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, updating, correction or deletion of your information, or withdraw a contact permission. Email diagnostico@sinecdo.com. We validate requests before modifying or disclosing records to reduce the risk of exposing information to third parties.

Controller

Operational controller: Sinecdo. Privacy and rights contact: diagnostico@sinecdo.com. Main operation: Argentina.
